# Salar Surgicals - Surgical Instruments Export Platform - architecture

A PHP/MySQL catalogue with an admin portal so staff maintain products without a developer, plus a structured quote-request flow that captures what the buyer actually needs. Product pages are built for search visibility, since export buyers arrive through search rather than direct traffic.

## Components

### Catalogue

Category and product browsing with structured attributes

### Admin portal

Product, category and enquiry management

### Quote flow

Structured request-for-quote capture

### Mail layer

Transactional email via PHPMailer

### Content pages

Policy, shipping and company information

## Stack

| Layer | Technology |
| --- | --- |
| Backend | PHP, MySQL |
| Frontend | Server-rendered pages |
| Email | PHPMailer |
| SEO | Structured product pages and sitemaps |
